---
title: "Abundance"
date: "2025-10-19"
type: "poetry"
excerpt: "Love can visit at the wrong time"
pullquote: "A gutter rat, beady eyes"
---


My lovers pearls are strewn
Across the road
They watch as I pick
The pieces up
Sewage
Colors my skin dark
In the corner of my eye
In the dark alley waters
A gutter rat, beady eyes
Pearl in mouth.
