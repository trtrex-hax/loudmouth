There's something about a woman in this faith
a resilience, a strength
tolerance 
strike me, lord
and don't do it lightly 
rip my clothes and beat me raw 
after all it is all I am good for 
still I will praise your name 
fall on my knees five times a day and lay prostate on the earth 
carry your words in my heart 
breathe in shards of glass 
prick my skin with needles every second of the day 
make me imperfect, then don't love me for it 
make me inferior 
God is god to man
man is god to woman 
there's something about this kind of faith 
something about a love with pain
something about suffering .
it feels religious if done right


~~~ Sada ~~~
