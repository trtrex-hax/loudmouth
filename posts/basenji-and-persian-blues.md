excerpt: "Are African Dogs Ugly?"
pullquote: "We dream of the meat we caught with our teeth."

"Are we only beautiful when we are born 
blue, double-coated and in the cold?"

There's a stunning beauty 
in the eyes of ancestors 
who don't shout.
Voice boxes, a bit too small 
for sights 
swallowed through teary eyes.

Relegation to the 
Edge of bustling dining halls,
Where everyone but us gets a seat.
Patient dogs dream 
of bones from the master's table. 
We dream of the meat we caught with our teeth.
We've been patient dogs for far too long.

I walk through dense forests
Hunter behind me, 
waiting to rip my rat 
out my mouth.
Feed his priced 
blue cat on the fat 
meat 
he peels off it
Toss me a couple measly bones
I dream of chase with 
the cat's blood, 
flooding my mouth.
