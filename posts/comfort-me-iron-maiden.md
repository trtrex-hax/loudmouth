---
title: "Comfort me, iron maiden"
date: "2026-02-24"
type: "poetry"
excerpt: "False Safeties"
pullquote: "For a second I think I can see the light"
---


I breathe with a puffed out chest.
And smile as a spike
Digs between my ribs.
I feel the warmth soak through my clothes 
And stick to the ends.
I squirm a little. 
Try to dig it deeper into my lungs
For a second I think I can see the light
In this dark box.
I'm good at that much.
It makes me a little happy.
