---
title: "Frayed edges."
date: "2025-08-01"
type: "poetry"
excerpt: "love, love, love"
pullquote: "It's always been out of my control"
---

I want to reach out
Touch the edges of your dress
Run my fingers through the 
overgrown parts of your old braids
As you fall asleep 
To movies
You begged we watched together.
I want you to see me
Open my chest up
Pull out my center ribs
I get the sense it's too late
It's always been out of my control
Your love, my love.
