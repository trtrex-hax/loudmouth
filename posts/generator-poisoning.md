---
title: "Generator Poisoning"
date: "2026-05-16"
type: "poetry"
excerpt: "Death can be a friend"
pullquote: "Your minds keep each other just a little warm"
---

There's something tender
about planning your 
death with someone else.
About the tiny honest giggles
The shared camaraderies.
That even as your eyes lose their spark
Your minds keep each other
just a little warm
You sit together,
noose in both hands as you talk about what it means 
to live, 
to live long enough to die, 
to live enough to die.
A short life is still a life.
We joke about finally getting to meet Sylvia Plath.
There's no heaven
Or hell
Just a coffee shop 
With a cute round table
And 2 black men
And a woman 
who might be just a little bit shocked
To see us there.
Now wouldn't that be lovely?
