Warm golden light filters through fishnet curtains
Settling on my skin like gold dust
Turned pomade.
My metal rectangle window takes me on many trips,
Stories of gods and 
the sons of mortal women
Fighting over life 
Across the night sky
Dance atop my eyelids
Like little rain drops 
Do, 
Settling inside one's ears.
with the 
pitter-patter of a world 
only gentle in its most obscure corners.
The late-afternoon sun is a white hot brand I long for
Floating lethargic against a light gray sky
As I,
glide lethargic through the
Dull white office walls
Where life is always a losing game.
Freedom is only born at the end of a setting sun
At the point where the sun
Has just begun 
To decree that Accra's traffic 
slow down
Seducing us with her most beautiful dress
Woven of light and warm color,
painting
The earth below with unimaginable radiance. 
We long for what lies outside the borders of our holding pens
Watch as we trickle out the 
mouth of the hope swallower 
Into the river of cars and bodies
Walking home.
But only when the sun is too far away to touch.
Mother's home is 
another prison on a new moon.
The late-afternoon sun stands, 
Waiting to mock us 
Domesticated animals
as we crawl home.
