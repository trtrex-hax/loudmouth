---
title: "In my sleep, I die."
date: "2026-01-30"
type: "poetry"
excerpt: "I write about death alot"
pullquote: "See what happens when a house is set ablaze"
---


When the devil
Chooses to make your hands
A cradle to usher his earthly form,
Makes them restless with the wanton need to 
see what happens when a house is set ablaze
See what color your blood is in black light
Or whether razors really break the skin
while your heart begs for calm
What do you do?

I turn my hands towards myself and begin to dig towards my chest.
My hands search tirelessly for a heart which screams in town squares.
I wrap the devil around my neck.
Watch as my legs stop kicking
Feel my hands trace the rivers buried in my heart.
Draw the warmth out of my body
Like fishermen pull up scores of fish.
I have never known peace this way
But the devil never escapes when I bury him in ancient rivers.
