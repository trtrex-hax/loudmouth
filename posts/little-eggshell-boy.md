---
title: "little eggshell boy"
date: "2026-05-01"
type: "poetry"
excerpt: "Something about the softness of becoming."
pullquote: "you can be both fragile and a container at the same time"
---

        i have been
              carried

carefully,
        yes —

but carried
the way you carry
        something
              you are not sure
                    will survive the trip.

                              *

        at some point
the hands stopped
              being careful

and i did not
        break

which surprised
        everyone,
                    including me.

i think i had confused
        softness
              for weakness

the way you confuse
        the shell
for the whole
        egg.
