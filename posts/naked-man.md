title: "Naked man"
date: "2026-02-07"
type: "poetry"
excerpt: "anxiety in a couple of lines"
pullquote: "I don't even feel safe with myself."

I don't even feel safe with myself.
So I open my windows
Stare at the people walking outside.
Tell myself today is the day 
I step out and talk like a normal person
Open myself up for others to look through my shelves, pick at me.
But by the time I am done with my sentence, my resolve is gone
My curtains have been drawn
My room is dark.
My coffee has gone cold.
