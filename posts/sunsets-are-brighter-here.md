title: "Sunsets are brighter here"
date: "2025-07-29"
type: "poetry"
excerpt: "the mosquitoes are more aggressive"
pullquote: "Steal brightness for personal skies."
---


It's nighttime in growing villages.
Places stuck between growth and staying in the ground.
I've been seated here for the last 3 hours.
Kelewele's scent serenades me.
The moon looks down at me.
Her eyes, loving in a way Accra's would never unmask.
I feel her love on my skin, silver laced, 
she drapes me.
The stars are tiny shells
Littering the black sky like the bones of the fish on Takoradi's beaches.
I catch myself reaching out to the sky
Asking the moon to draw nearer
Trying to pick the stars from the sky
Place them in pockets,
Steal brightness for personal skies.
I hadn't realized this until a second ago.
In the places closest to home,
Even the end of good things are beautiful.
