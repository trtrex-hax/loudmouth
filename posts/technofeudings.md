More than half of the internet's traffic is bots
more than half–
of the Internet can be bought
your taste has probably been bought
your opinions can be bought
your favorite musician probably only has half of their streams
backlash might not be what we thought.

the trials of the court of public opinion can be bought
Amber Heard lost even though she's also the victim
Israel will win, even after killing children
we can't ever fight, the oligarchs are winning
we could never collectivize, the bots are within
you could never unionize, where the fuck are we meeting?
Your children are born a minority, with their 
odd comments that go against the bot consensus…
to fight against the structures will always be a losing game.
we'll never have a new free thought in this new age we are entering.
We are entering…
Meaning?
you might make the right choices, but what about the 50 percent +1
