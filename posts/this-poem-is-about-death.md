My uncle says I've always been obsessed with death.
"Why do you care so much about how someone dies, much more as a child. Anyway..."
The only memories I have of him 
a disembodied voice,
A picture of us playing basketball,
An old house and hotdogs I am not quite certain he bought.
A man who smiled a bit too wide.
I killed the other memories.
Today,
I daydream of where my "ego" will take me.
About why so many men die of suicide
When women attempt more.
I wonder what a dead person looks like from 5 feet away.
As I walk up to an open casket
My aunt/therapist is dead.
I regret not talking with her,
She takes the last parts of my mind when she retreats to the earth.
I remember telling
my parents 
I feared who I would be if I stood by death in the pews. 
I think he is sitting in the car as we go home.
Do lonely, cold men reject a hand
Because it is cold?
I wish I asked how 
To heal
my inner child.
Children should not have to worry about death.
